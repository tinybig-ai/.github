# Contributing

Thank you for your help.

Most tinybig repositories are private. The jep repository is open source, and
jep is the place for contributions.

- Read the jep contributing guide first:
  https://github.com/tinybig-ai/jep/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md. The guide gives
  the setup, the change process, and the rules.
- If you are new and you want a first task, look at the good first issues:
  https://github.com/tinybig-ai/jep/labels/good%20first%20issue.
- Send a security problem to SECURITY.md. Do not open a public issue.

This guide is the default for repositories that have no guide of their own.
